Featured Case Study

Phoebestar Royalty School

Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria · Primary & Secondary · 700+ students · 75 teachers

The Problem

Inconsistent intervention tracking across grade levels. Teachers identified struggling students at different rates using different criteria. Intervention effectiveness was not systematically measured. The school had assessment data but no structured way to translate that data into action.

System Applied

  • IDEOGRID Performance Tracking Layer — configured for their assessment framework
  • IDEOGRID Intervention Management System — customized with their intervention menu
  • IDEOGRID Teacher Insight Dashboard — deployed to all 42 teachers
