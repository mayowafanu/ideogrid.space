About

IDEOGRID is developed and maintained by Learning Systems Lab — a research group dedicated to designing decision-support systems for education.

Why This Lab Exists

Classrooms are complex adaptive systems. Teachers face overwhelming data streams, fragmented tools, and a critical absence of structured decision frameworks. Most educational technology responds by adding more data. We build interpretation systems instead.

The lab's research examines how teachers actually make instructional decisions — and designs systems that make those decisions more consistent, more timely, and more effective.

Research Philosophy

  • Structure over raw data
  • Interpretation over dashboards
  • Systems over tools

Relationship Map

Research Lab → IDEOGRID System → Schools

Research generates ideas. IDEOGRID operationalizes them. Case studies validate them. The cycle feeds back — classroom evidence informs the next research question.
